📱 PDA Network & Radio Frequencies
☢️ RADIATION (total Rads)
  • 0-2: No additional condition.
  • 3-4: Nausea. Stamina and Survival -1.
  • 5-6: Sickness. Physical Skills -1; natural Health recovery halved.
  • 7-8: Severe sickness. Physical Skills -2; +1 Stress each Shift.
  • 9+: Critical exposure. Each new Rad triggers Panic and may cause lasting injury.

Whenever you gain a Rad, roll Base Dice equal to your total Rads; each success = 1 Damage.

🧱 COVER (counts as extra Armor)
  • Brush / concealment: 1
  • Furniture / rubble: 2
  • Vehicle / heavy debris: 3
  • Brick / concrete wall: 4
  • Reinforced structure: 5+

Armor roll: Base Dice = Armor Rating; each success reduces Damage by 1. Armor does not stack with other armor.

⚡ STRESS, PUSH & PANIC
  • Push: once, after failing a roll. +1 Stress, then reroll every die that is not a success.
  • Panic Roll (a 1 on a Stress Die): D6 + Stress.
    1-6 Keep it together · 7 Nervous twitch · 8 Tremble (-2 Agility) · 9 Drop item · 10 Freeze · 11 Seek cover · 12 Flee · 13 Blind panic · 14+ Break
⚠️ CONDITIONS SUMMARY
🍞 Starving: No normal Health recovery or Stress relief. Meal + one Shift removes it.
💧 Dehydrated: Physical activity harder; rest cannot relieve Stress. Water + one Shift removes it.
💤 Exhausted: Agility and Wits Skills -1, Mobility a further -1. One Shift of sleep removes it.
❄️ Cold: Mobility and Survival -1. Warm, dry shelter removes it.
🥶 Hypothermia: Physical Skills -2; 1 Damage per Shift in severe cold. Needs Medical Aid and warmth.
🩸 Bleeding: Lose 1 Health each Round. Bandage, Tourniquet or Medical Aid stops it.
☠️ Poisoned: Usually Stamina -2 and 1 Damage per Shift. Antidote, Medical Aid or time removes it.
☣️ Radiation Sickness: See the Radiation card.
🔧 GEAR, REPAIR & AMMO
  • Item Conditions: Good (no penalty) ➔ Worn (no immediate penalty) ➔ Damaged (-1 to rolls using it) ➔ Broken (unusable).
  • Repair: Heavy Machinery for mechanical, Comtech for electronic. Field Heavy Machinery repairs need a Repair kit; a workshop and parts restore Worn to Good.
  • Ammo: Standard (STD). Hollow-point (HP): +1 Damage against an unarmored human target. Armor-piercing (AP): halves the target's Armor Rating, rounded down.
